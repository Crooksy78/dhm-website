Your People Grow The Business. AI Should Give Them Time To Do It.

Most teams lose hours every week to work that adds nothing. Done properly, AI takes that work off their plate, and the time comes back as more sales, better service and lower costs.

The Hard Part Isn't The AI.
It's Knowing Which Work To Hand Over.

Every business has a handful of jobs that quietly eat the week. Quotes that take a day to turn around. Invoices typed in by hand. The same report rebuilt every month. The same customer question answered for the fifth time.

AI can help with most of them. But they're not all worth the money. Pick the wrong one first and you end up with a subscription nobody uses, and a team that's gone off the whole idea.

So the first step isn't buying anything. It's working out which job is costing you the most, and whether AI can actually fix it.

That's what the 90-minute session is for.

The 90-Minute Strategy Session

You and whoever runs the day-to-day sit down with me: your ops lead, office manager or bookkeeper. We don't talk about code or the latest AI headlines. We talk about your processes, the hours they take, the mistakes they cause, and what all that costs you.

The goal is profit: more revenue, lower costs, or more done by the team you already have. Not fewer people.

What You Walk Away With

01

Your Week, Mapped

The repetitive jobs that came up in the session, with a rough count of the hours each one takes. Retyping, chasing, reporting, answering the same question twice.

02

The Jobs Worth Handing To AI

The two or three where AI is most likely to pay for itself, each with an hours-and-dollars estimate built from what you've told me. Some may already be sitting in software you pay for.

03

Where To Start, And What To Leave

The one I'd do first, and why. Plus the jobs I'd keep with a person, because not everything should go to AI.

It all comes to you as one plain-English plan within five business days.
