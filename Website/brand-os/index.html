Brand OS

The Brand Operating System

A permanent foundation layer for Claude Code & Claude Cowork. Everything Claude needs to know about your business, so what comes back sounds like you wrote it. Set up once, read automatically on every task, by everyone on your team.

What it is

Three Files. Everything Claude Needs to Know About Your Brand.

The Brand OS is a set of three structured files that capture what your business does, how it communicates, and who it sells to. That knowledge lives inside your Claude workspace and is read automatically before anything is produced.

The Business business.md

What The Business Is

The factual brief: positioning, what makes the business different, hero products or services, the competitive landscape, proof points. Written the way you'd brief a new marketing hire on their first day.

The Voice voice.md

How The Brand Actually Sounds

Captured from real patterns in real content. Not adjectives like "bold and confident." Sentence structures, rhythm, the words the brand uses, the words it never uses, and concrete before-and-after rewrites that show Claude what the voice looks like in practice.

The Customer customer.md

The Customer In Their Own Language

Built from real reviews, real conversations, and real research — not invented personas. Core pains, objections, buying triggers, and the phrases customers use to describe their own problems.

What changes in practice

The Output Stops Drifting. The Brand Stays Consistent.

Without a Brand OS, every session starts cold.

Claude fills the gaps with its own best guess, the average of everything it's ever seen, which is never quite your brand.

So the output drifts: from one person to the next, from one week to the next.

Once the three files are in place, every task starts from the same context. The drift stops, and consistency no longer depends on who's at the keyboard.

Consistent output across the team

Everyone produces work in the same voice, regardless of experience level or how long they've been there.

Better first drafts

Claude starts brand-aware, so there's less editing and less time spent putting the brand back in before anything's usable.

Brand knowledge that stays

What currently lives in one person's head lives in the system instead. It holds when someone goes on leave, a contractor rolls off, or a new person comes in.

A system that compounds

Every Skill you build on top of it extends its value. The Brand OS is the foundation, and foundations appreciate.

FAQs

Common Questions

Why Claude Code / Cowork?

Because they work with real files on your machine, not just a chat window. Claude in the browser starts from nothing every time and you paste context in by hand. Claude Code and Cowork read your Brand OS straight off your computer, so every task starts brand-aware without anyone having to remember to load anything. That's the whole reason the system holds up in practice.

Can I Use This With ChatGPT or Gemini?

The files themselves are useful anywhere — you could load them into a custom GPT or a Gemini Gem and get better output than without them. We build and support this in Claude because we think it's the best option for small businesses getting real work done.

Do I Need to Pay for Claude?

Yes. The Brand OS runs inside Claude Code or Cowork, both of which need a paid Claude plan. It's a modest monthly cost next to what the system saves a team in time and gives you access to the more advanced features of Claude.

Is My Business Information Safe in Claude?

Reasonable question, since you're handing over positioning, customer language, and reviews. On a paid plan, whether your conversations are used to improve Claude's models is a setting you control: switch it off in your privacy settings and they're not used for training. Anthropic's business and team plans exclude that by default. Beyond that, most of what goes into a Brand OS is brand context rather than sensitive data, and much of it is already public. Your files live in your own workspace, under your control. If you have genuinely sensitive material, we keep it out of the files.
