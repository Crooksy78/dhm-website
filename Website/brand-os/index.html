What it is

Three Files. Everything Claude Needs to Know About Your Brand.

The Brand OS is a set of three structured files that capture what your business does, how it communicates, and who it sells to. That knowledge lives inside your Claude workspace and is read automatically before anything is produced.

The Businessthe-business.md

What the Business Is

The factual brief: positioning, what makes the business different, hero products or services, the competitive landscape, proof points. Written the way you'd brief a new marketing hire on their first day.

The Voicethe-voice.md

How the Brand Actually Sounds

Captured from real patterns in real content. Not adjectives like "bold and confident." Sentence structures, rhythm, the words the brand uses, the words it never uses, and concrete before-and-after rewrites that show Claude what the voice looks like in practice.

The Customerthe-customer.md

The Customer in Their Own Language

Built from real reviews, real conversations, and real research — not invented personas. Core pains, objections, buying triggers, and the phrases customers use to describe their own problems.

FAQs

Common Questions

Why Claude Cowork?

Claude Cowork is designed as a shared team workspace, and it's the cleanest way to make foundation files available automatically to every task. It's built around an agent model, which means Skills run on top of your Brand OS without anyone having to load context manually each time. Everyone on the team works from the same foundation, every session, without thinking about it.

Can I Use This With ChatGPT or Gemini?

The system is built Claude-native, and that's deliberate. Claude's ability to read foundation files and run Skills consistently is core to how the Brand OS holds up in practice. The same files pasted into another tool won't behave the same way — the agent layer that reads them automatically isn't there. If you're committed to ChatGPT or Gemini, this probably isn't the right fit, and I'd say so rather than sell you something that won't perform.

Do I Need to Pay for Claude?

Yes. The Brand OS runs inside Claude Cowork, which needs a paid Claude plan. It's a modest monthly cost next to what the system saves a team in time and gives you access to the more advanced features of Claude.

Is My Business Information Safe in Claude?

On Pro plans and above — which is what Cowork requires — you control what Claude can do with your data, and Anthropic does not use your conversations to train its models. Most of what goes into a Brand OS is brand context rather than sensitive data, and much of it is already public. Your files live in your own workspace, under your control. If you have genuinely sensitive material, we keep it out of the files.
