Where Is Your Data Safe?

Not all AI plans are equal. The tier you choose determines how your data is protected.

Free & Individual
$0–20/mo
  • Data may be used for model training
  • Human reviewers may see your input
  • Consumer terms of service only
ChatGPT Free/Plus, Gemini Free/Advanced, Claude Free/Pro
Team / Business
$25–30/user/mo
  • Data NOT used for training
  • Business data protections and admin controls
  • SOC 2 compliance
ChatGPT Team, Claude Team, M365 Copilot, Workspace Gemini
Enterprise
Custom pricing
  • Contractual data guarantees
  • Custom retention, SSO, audit logs
  • Australian Privacy Act compliance
ChatGPT Enterprise, Claude Enterprise, Vertex AI
The minimum for sensitive employee data: Team/Business tier at $25+/user/month.

Your AI Policy Can Start on One Page

You don't need a 20-page governance document. Cover these five things.

01

Approved Tools

Which AI tools are allowed? Which are not?

02

Prohibited Data

What can never go into an AI tool? (personal information, employment records, legal matters)

03

Required Review

All AI output must be reviewed by a human before use.

04

Documentation

When AI is used for decisions, it should be noted.

05

Transparency

Employees should know when AI was used in processes that affect them.
