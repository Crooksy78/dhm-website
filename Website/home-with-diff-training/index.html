What We Can Help You With

Training shaped around where you're starting from and what you need.

Fundamentals

The shift from chatbot to agent: how Claude Code actually works, how it's different to working with Claude in the browser, and why that difference matters.

Driving It

Briefing properly. Getting it to show you the plan before it executes. Reading the output and steering mid-task. Sessions, context, files in and out. The skills that make the difference between a neat demo and something you actually use every day.

Building Systems

Your Brand OS, captured once. Everything about your business, your customers, how you write. Built into a system that runs the same way every time, by anyone on your team.

Marketing Applications

Content pipelines. Ad copy at scale. Email campaigns. Monthly reports that write themselves. Research that verifies instead of invents. Images and video. Decks and spreadsheets. Everything from briefing to finished, live.

Connecting Your Stack

Your real tools: Google Drive, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Shopify, Klaviyo, Analytics, whatever you actually use. Making them talk to Claude Code so the work flows across systems instead of stopping at spreadsheet exports.

Running It Unattended

Scheduling jobs to run overnight. Setting up automations so things work while you sleep. Running multiple things at once instead of waiting for one to finish. The moves that actually change what one person can handle.

Team & Governance

Brand consistency across operators. Approval workflows. Client data safety. Onboarding a new person without the founder. What changes when it's more than just you.

Keeping It Alive

When something's not working, how to diagnose it. Updating your systems as the business changes. Staying current without chasing every announcement. Building your next system outside marketing.
